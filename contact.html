01 GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Do I need a fully formed project brief?+

No. A challenge, an early idea or a process you want to improve is enough for a first conversation.

Can we start with a small project?+

Yes. A focused first step can help clarify what is useful before expanding the work.

What happens after I send a note?+

Once BITS’s public contact address is configured, the form will open an email draft for you to review and send. Until then, it will clearly tell you that no message was sent.
